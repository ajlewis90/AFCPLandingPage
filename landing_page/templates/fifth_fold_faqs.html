Frequently Asked Questions

How do we ensure reliability, and safety around products being listed and sold? +

Our platform ensures that there are extremely low chances of online shoppers identifying fake products or fake reviews or even false advertising. Our vetting platform and team perform regular checks to ensure that products listed by retailers are legit and safe. Moreover, we have implemented many guardrails around grounding and safety that could even reject products after listing by human reviewers if necessary.

Can AFCP integrate with our existing systems and tools? +

Yes, absolutely. AFCP makes life easy for you and does not replace your toolset. Rather, our platform provides you an easy-to-use interface that reduces manual effort by removing friction at various touchpoints. Your customers can shop effortlessly, never miss a deal, get timely updates, and great suggestions.

What are the transaction fees for payments? +

Payment fees are handled by third-parties and are separate from our service pricing. So there is a small fee associated for this and is part of the commission per sale.

How do credits work? +

Credits are used to power our agents and their interactions with your customers. Each conversation, product recommendation, and Agent-powered action consumes credits based on complexity. So, queries of a complex nature need more processing and therefore, consume higher credits. Credits are included in your monthly plan and can be purchased additionally as needed.
