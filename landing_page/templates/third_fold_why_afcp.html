Why choose AFCP AI?

AI lacks magic and interactivity. Token processing implemented by model-first technology is a nightmare. We can change this if we focus on building Agents with a superior experience. The whole world will eventually use Agents instead of clicking, searching, or typing. Most digital experiences will be Agent-driven. We are building technology to enable this shift.

We are building an Agentic platform that helps online retailers and consumers transition to an Agent-driven world. Businesses create Agents for their customers and use our platform to automate their operational tasks

The problem

Maintaining different code bases and systems for your web, desktop and mobile e-commerce store is a nightmare. It strains your budget and team.

It also costs you sales. When your customers have to click, type and search their way across a different experience on every device, they get inconsistent shopping experiences: missing a deal or promo on mobile that appeared on your website, or losing a cart when they switch from tablet to desktop. These fragmented, cumbersome experiences feel impersonal, and shopping with your brand stops being something customers enjoy.

The solution

Why build different solutions for mobile, desktop and tablet when one agent can do the job? We believe a single shopper agent can be built to deliver consistent experiences everywhere your customers are, present with them across every device, remembering their preferences and their cart no matter where they pick up.

Our solution helps businesses create Shopper Agents with a no-code approach and flexibility for personalization. We empower e-commerce retailers to deploy a shopper agent that unlocks genuine connection and reliable shopping experiences for their customers. This agent lives with their customers wherever they are.

Using our Agentic OS, enterprises can turn brittle, fragmented stacks into effortless, unified experiences. They no longer need multiple apps or distinct code bases to handle retail operations like product listing, pricing, inventory restocking and demand forecasting. The need for multi-platform code bases evaporates.
